About

Sudha Murthy is a celebrated Indian author, philanthropist, and social worker. She has written numerous books in both English and Kannada, including novels, short stories, travelogues, and children’s literature. Her writing is known for its simplicity, moral values, and ability to inspire readers of all ages. Sudha Murthy is also the chairperson of the Infosys Foundation, through which she has contributed extensively to education, healthcare, rural development, and welfare for underprivileged communities. She has built schools, libraries, and hospitals in rural areas, providing opportunities for the marginalized sections of society. Her work emphasizes the importance of literacy, women’s empowerment, and social responsibility. Sudha Murthy has received several awards for her literary contributions and humanitarian work. She is admired for her humility, compassion, and dedication to improving society. Through her books and public initiatives, she continues to inspire generations to pursue knowledge, kindness, and service to others. Even today, she actively participates in social projects and shares her life experiences, teaching valuable lessons about simplicity, ethics, and perseverance. Her achievements make her a role model for students, aspiring writers, and social workers across India and beyond.
