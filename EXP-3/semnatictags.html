His Journey

N. R. Narayana Murthy is an Indian billionaire businessman and the co-founder of Infosys, a global leader in IT services. Known as the "Father of the Indian IT Sector," he played a crucial role in putting India on the world technology map.

Under his leadership, Infosys became synonymous with corporate governance, innovation, and ethical business practices. His vision transformed the IT landscape of India and created countless opportunities for young professionals.

Beyond business, Narayana Murthy is a philanthropist, supporting education, healthcare, and social initiatives. His leadership style and humility continue to inspire entrepreneurs and leaders worldwide.
